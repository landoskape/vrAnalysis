Reading the results

  • The robust performance summary and the all-fold additive account answer different questions. The median-fold G+E score averages 0.1245 across mice; its all-fold pooled counterpart is 0.0416. In the pooled account, the gain increment is -0.0624, driven downward by rare poor gain predictions. E still adds +0.0134 on that same pooled denominator.
  • E provides additional prediction beyond the reused gain model. Its pooled-over-fold original-activity R² increment is 0.0134 internally and 0.0159 externally, using all units and equal-mouse weighting. The increment is positive in 264/267 internal units and 262/267 external units.
  • The combined model still falls short of full peer RRR. Mean median-fold R² is 0.1245 for G+E, versus 0.1578 for peer RRR. The 28.9% gap recovery is a ratio of these aggregate differences, not an estimate of a biological variance fraction or maximum explainable predictability.
  • The regularized measured E is nearly off-template, although not constrained to be. Its template-shaped energy fraction averages 0.072% internally and 0.023% externally.
  • Predicted E contains a larger, but still minority, template-shaped component. The corresponding fractions are 4.34% and 4.69%. Energy fractions alone do not tell us what fraction of the incremental predictive improvement is gain-related: that would require separately scoring the projected and off-template prediction components.
  • The E branch is comparatively high rank. Median internal gain rank is 22; median residual rank is 533. The residual branch has framewise observations and a much higher rank bound.

Matching, weighting, and limits

All six compared model results are matched on session, environment identity, source and target ROI IDs, and the exact trial folds. Internal G-only also matches the standalone gain model's saved PF/gain hyperparameters and fold scores within numerical tolerance. Cached summaries do not include every retained prediction-frame index, so exact cross-model frame support cannot be independently audited here. Within the new model G and E share the same scoring frames.

The 9 saved sessions without a finite scored environment remain listed in session_coverage.csv. All scored units and folds are retained, including negative scores. No score-based subset, winsorization, or outlier clipping is used. Across-mouse summaries first average environments within a session and sessions within each mouse. No interval treats sessions or folds as independent animals.

Hyperparameters use a fixed validation quarter and are reused across the three rotations. Place-cell selection uses full-session activity. Internal position is decoded from source cells; trial gains use the source trial's frames. E may include template mismatch and position-decoding error as well as other shared activity structure. Component R² values have component-specific denominators; only the original-activity SSE increments provide the additive account shown in Figure 1D.
