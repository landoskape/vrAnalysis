What the saved results support

  • There is predictable structure beyond a per-trial template rescaling. Measured E remains after an explicit per-cell, per-trial template projection. Its component R² is positive in 262/267 internal units and 263/267 external units. These are descriptive counts; folds and units share animals.
  • The gain predictor is the current bottleneck. Failures affect 115/267 internal units and 61/267 external units. G-only and G+E errors track each other in the severe tail. The worst internal fold has R² = -2.06e+98.
  • Median-fold scoring hides some failures. Only 50 internal units have median R² < −1, although 115 have at least one such fold. Pooled increments also lose precision when subtracting enormous, nearly equal SSEs, so we do not interpret their numerical values in that tail.
  • Residual prediction is not necessarily low dimensional. The median internal residual rank is 528, versus 16 for gain. E has many more observations and a much higher available-rank bound.

Interpretation and next step

Predictable E can include template mismatch and, for the internal model, position-decoding errors. The result establishes structure beyond this chosen trial-gain template; it does not by itself identify a separate physiological process.

The failure pattern is consistent with ill-conditioned raw gain ratios when a cell's template energy is tiny. The new decomposition uses unregularized gains to preserve exact orthogonality; the existing structured-gain model shrinks gain measurements toward 1. That is a plausible explanation from the implementation and G-only error pattern, not a direct measurement of failing template energies.

A useful repair would preserve the explicit measured decomposition while stabilizing the gain features used by the learned predictor (for example, with separate regularized source-gain features). The branches should then be reevaluated before claiming a clean additive result. This report does not modify the model, delete results, or refit sessions.

Data and reproducibility

All six compared models match on session, environment identity, exact source/target ROI IDs, and trial-fold membership. Every compared model has 149 saved sessions; 140 sessions have at least one finite scored environment (267 scored units in total). The other 9 sessions contribute no scored environments and are listed in session_coverage.csv. Exact retained prediction-frame masks are absent from cached summaries; cross-model frame support cannot be independently audited here. Within the new model, G and E share a frame mask.

The main comparison uses the median of three held-out trial-fold scores. The validation quarter is fixed and hyperparameters are reused across rotations. Place-cell selection uses full-session activity, as in the existing analysis. There is no noise-ceiling correction: full peer RRR is a benchmark, not maximum explainable predictability.
