Follow-up: Activity levels, participation identity, and familiarity — eight figures

149 stored v9 sessions, 14 mice, 267 session/environment combinations. 27 figures. Configuration: pfpred_quality_spks=sigrebase_rel=0.3_fa=0.1_v9.

This is a scratch analysis of cached results, with no new formal plotters. Black / blue / green mean first / second / third environment experienced by each mouse, rather than a common raw environment ID. Within-environment session numbers count recorded sessions where that slot was present. There is no cross-session ROI tracking.

Quality-filtered cells require spatial reliability >0.3 AND fraction active >0.1; complement is every other ROI. This differs from a reliability-only place-cell cutoff of 0.7. Amplitude CV is standard deviation / mean across trials, not a correlation reliability score. Higher positive CV means more trial-to-trial variability. Tiny or signed means can make CV unstable; inspect medians, means, tails and finite coverage together.

Trial RMS and mean activity summarize across position; PF projection uses the unit-L2 all-trial place field; Gaussian gain uses the fitted Gaussian field. Means/variances/CVs summarize across trials. These are descriptive amplitude measures from all-trial maps; their relationships to spatial metrics may share data and algebraic dependencies. Normalized RMS and R2 are mechanically related (R2 = 1 - normalized RMS squared, where defined); PF variance fraction is prediction variance / activity variance, not explained variance.

Only the 22 metrics available per environment slot enter the 231-pair atlas. Older best-environment-only activity-shape and correlation statistics are excluded from that atlas to keep ROI/environment matching consistent.

In this cache the median Gaussian gain is essentially 1; its median experience panel uses a fixed 0.95-1.05 scale to avoid magnifying numerical differences. Spearman correlations involving nearly constant gain means can rank tiny numerical fluctuations and should not be interpreted as biological effects. Cached v9 results span computation dates; this report reads them as stored, without recomputing them with the current source.
